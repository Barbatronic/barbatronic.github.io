Mechatronics engineer, first in the railway industry, then in FabLabs. For more than ten years
I have been building, teaching and sharing what I learn.

## What I do

- **System design**: mechanics, electronics and embedded code designed together, from idea to working object.
- **Prototyping**: 3D printing, laser cutting, milling, moulding, PCBs. The right technology for each part.
- **Embedded electronics**: boards, microcontrollers, sensors, actuator control.
- **Robotics**: my favourite playground, with the [Karibous](https://leskaribous.fr) competition robots (French Robotics Cup) and home-made robots.
- **Teaching**: classes, MakerSpace, videos and live streams on YouTube and Twitch.

## What I do

- **Robotics**: competition robots with [Les Karibous](https://leskaribous.fr) (French Robotics Cup) and home-made RC robots.
- **3D printing**: functional parts, workshop tooling.
- **Electronics**: boards, microcontrollers, ESP32, actuator control.
- **Teaching**: classes, MakerSpace, videos and live streams.
